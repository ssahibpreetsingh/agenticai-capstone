APPOINTMENT AND SCHEDULING POLICY

1. Booking Window
Routine follow-up appointments can be booked up to 60 days in advance. Urgent
appointments (flagged by triage as needing review within 48 hours) are booked into a
same-day or next-day reserved slot pool.

2. Cancellation Policy
Patients should cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours in advance where possible.
Repeated late cancellations (fewer than 24 hours' notice) or no-shows may result in a
review of future booking priority, though care is never denied on this basis for
urgent needs.

3. Follow-Up Scheduling for Chronic Conditions
Patients on chronic disease management plans (e.g., Type 2 Diabetes) are typically
scheduled for follow-up every 3 months during active titration of medication, moving to
every 6 months once stable at target, per the relevant clinical guideline.

4. Lab Results Turnaround
Routine lab results are typically available within 3–5 working days and are reviewed
by the ordering clinician before being released to the patient portal. Urgent/flagged
results are reviewed and communicated to the patient within 24 hours.

5. Telehealth vs In-Person
Follow-up appointments for stable chronic conditions may be conducted via telehealth
where clinically appropriate. New patient assessments, physical examinations, and any
appointment following an abnormal lab result generally require an in-person visit.
