FEE STRUCTURE — RETAIL BANKING PRODUCTS

Account Maintenance
- Savings account: No minimum balance fee for accounts with average monthly balance
  above INR 10,000. Below this, a shortfall fee of INR 400/month applies.
- Current account: Minimum average balance of INR 25,000 required; shortfall fee of
  INR 1,000/month applies below this threshold.

Card Fees
- Debit card annual fee: INR 250 + GST, waived in the first year.
- Credit card annual fee: Varies by card tier (INR 500 to INR 5,000), often waived on
  reaching an annual spend threshold specific to each card variant.
- ATM withdrawal: First 5 transactions per month free at own-bank ATMs; INR 21 per
  transaction thereafter. Other-bank ATMs: 3 free transactions per month, INR 21
  thereafter.

Loan-Related Fees
- Processing fee: See loan_policy.txt, Section 4.
- Foreclosure charge: See loan_policy.txt, Section 5.
- Late payment charge: 2% of overdue EMI amount plus GST.
- Cheque bounce charge: INR 500 per instance.

Fund Transfer
- NEFT: Free for amounts up to INR 10,000; tiered charges above this (INR 5 to INR 25
  per transaction depending on slab).
- RTGS: INR 25 to INR 50 per transaction depending on amount slab.
- IMPS: INR 5 to INR 15 per transaction depending on amount slab.
