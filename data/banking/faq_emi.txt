FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS — EMI AND REPAYMENT

Q: Can I postpone my EMI (instalment deferral)?
A: Instalment deferral is available for customers facing genuine financial hardship,
subject to approval. Customers may request deferral of up to 2 EMIs per loan tenure.
Interest continues to accrue during the deferral period, and the tenure is extended
accordingly. Deferral requests must be submitted at least 5 working days before the
due EMI date.

Q: What happens if I miss an EMI payment?
A: A missed EMI attracts a late payment charge of 2% of the overdue amount plus
applicable GST. If an EMI remains unpaid for more than 90 days, the account is
classified as a Non-Performing Asset (NPA) and reported to credit bureaus, which may
significantly impact the customer's credit score.

Q: Can I change my EMI due date?
A: Yes, the EMI due date can be changed once per loan tenure, subject to a processing
fee of INR 500. Requests must be submitted through the customer portal or branch.

Q: How is the EMI amount calculated?
A: EMI is calculated using the reducing balance method based on principal, interest
rate, and tenure. See the Loan Policy document (loan_policy.txt) for the exact formula.

Q: Can I make a part-payment to reduce my EMI or tenure?
A: Yes, part-payments reduce the outstanding principal. Customers can choose to either
reduce the EMI amount (keeping tenure the same) or reduce the tenure (keeping EMI the
same) at the time of part-payment.
