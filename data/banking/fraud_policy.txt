FRAUD AND UNAUTHORISED TRANSACTION POLICY

1. Reporting Window
Customers must report any unauthorised or suspicious transaction within 3 working days
of receiving the transaction notification to be eligible for zero-liability protection.
Reports made between 4 and 7 working days may still be eligible for limited liability
protection, subject to investigation. Reports made after 7 working days are handled on
a case-by-case basis with no guaranteed reimbursement.

2. Zero Liability Conditions
Zero liability applies when the unauthorised transaction results from a third-party
breach not attributable to the customer (e.g., a data breach at a merchant, SIM
cloning) and the customer reports it within the window above and cooperates fully with
the investigation.

3. Investigation Process
Upon report, the transaction is provisionally reversed (a "shadow credit") while
investigation proceeds. Investigations typically conclude within 10 working days. If
fraud is confirmed, the shadow credit becomes permanent. If the transaction is found to
be authorised or the customer is found negligent (e.g., shared OTP/PIN), the shadow
credit is reversed.

4. Card Blocking
Customers should immediately block the affected card via the mobile app, customer care
line, or SMS banking upon noticing suspicious activity, even before filing the formal
dispute.

5. Required Documentation
- Written complaint (via app, portal, or branch) describing the transaction
- Any communication received from the fraudster (SMS, email, call log) if available
- Police complaint / FIR copy, required for amounts above INR 1,00,000
