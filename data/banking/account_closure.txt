ACCOUNT CLOSURE POLICY

1. Voluntary Closure
Customers may request account closure at any branch or via the customer portal.
Accounts closed within 14 days of opening ("cooling-off period") incur no closure
charge. Accounts closed between 14 days and 12 months of opening incur a closure
charge of INR 500. No charge applies after 12 months.

2. Documentation Required
- Original passbook / unused chequebook leaves (if applicable)
- Signed closure request form
- Valid government-issued ID for verification

3. Outstanding Dues
All outstanding dues, including any linked loan EMIs, credit card balances, or
negative balances, must be cleared before closure is processed. Accounts with active
loans linked as collateral cannot be closed until the loan is fully repaid or
transferred.

4. Processing Time
Standard closure requests are processed within 7 working days. Closure requests
involving a linked Fixed Deposit or active loan may take up to 15 working days due to
additional verification.

5. Dormant Account Closure
Accounts with no customer-initiated transactions for 24 consecutive months are
classified as dormant. Dormant accounts with a zero balance for 12 additional months
may be closed by the bank after written notice to the customer's last known address
and registered email.
