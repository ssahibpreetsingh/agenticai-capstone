PERSONAL LOAN POLICY — FICTIONAL BANK (v3.2)

1. Eligibility
Applicants must be salaried or self-employed individuals aged 21 to 60 years, with a
minimum monthly net income of INR 25,000 for salaried applicants or annual turnover of
INR 12,00,000 for self-employed applicants. A minimum credit score of 700 is required
for standard approval; applicants with a score between 650 and 699 may be considered
under the Secondary Review process with additional documentation.

2. Loan Amount and Tenure
Personal loans range from INR 50,000 to INR 25,00,000. Repayment tenure ranges from 12
to 60 months. The maximum loan amount is capped at 20 times the applicants average
monthly net income.

3. Interest Rates
Interest rates range from 10.5% to 18% per annum, determined by credit score, income
stability, and existing debt-to-income ratio. Rates are fixed for the full tenure and
do not change after disbursement.

4. Processing Fee
A one-time processing fee of 1.5% of the loan amount (minimum INR 1,000, maximum INR
15,000) is charged at disbursement. This fee is non-refundable once the loan is
disbursed, but is fully refunded if the application is rejected.

5. Prepayment and Foreclosure
Loans may be foreclosed after 6 EMIs have been paid. A foreclosure charge of 3% of the
outstanding principal applies. Partial prepayment is allowed up to twice per year with
no charge, provided each prepayment is at least 2 EMIs worth of principal.

6. EMI Calculation
EMI is calculated using the standard reducing-balance formula:
EMI = P x r x (1+r)^n / ((1+r)^n - 1)
where P = principal, r = monthly interest rate (annual rate / 12 / 100), n = tenure in
months.

7. Application Window
Loan applications remain open for processing for 90 days from the date of submission.
Applications not completed (missing documents, no response to queries) within this
window are automatically closed and must be resubmitted.
